## The store tool
`K() { python3 -B {GENERIC}/tools/knowledge_v2/build_knowledge_v2.py "$@" --pack {PACK}/<pack>.json --dir {STORE}; }` (every K call gets the seat's pack file and store folder) (a function: a zsh variable holding several words is one word). Every write: `K op --who CUR<run> ...`; never a hand edit (OPS_LOG.tsv logs each op). The tool sets every status from numbers (status, status_rule, n_runs, n_mechanisms); you never set one by judgement.

## Steps
1. Evidence: one EVIDENCE op per row of ops/EVIDENCE_HANDOFF.tsv (each graded round, each split gap; a row already in the store is not filed twice (the YARDSTICK row when its job is in {STORE}/EVIDENCE.tsv, REF rows that name an E id); a PLACEBO row of round differences has no t: its numbers go in the notes): `$K op --op EVIDENCE --set run= arm= mechanism= test_kind=<the row's test_kind> lead15= minus_fdonly15=<the row's minus_yard15 as the hand-off gives it, on every row, splits too> t= waves= job= source_file=`. A control run (arm C) stops here: evidence rows only, then all of step 7 (check, brief, pooled check) and step 8.
2. Findings (memory runs): ADD (new), UPDATE (append eids, next_test), MERGE or RETIRE (superseded_by); never rewrite FINDINGS.tsv; each finding cites at least one eid. A test against a finding (no separation, or the opposite sign) is filed on that finding like a success. The tool's rules (the pack's status_bars; alarm pack example: refuted_min_runs 2, t_bar 2.5): REFUTED after refuted_min_runs runs without separation or an opposite t >= t_bar (it then shows as DO NOT RETRY); CONFIRMED only through the orchestrator's HOLDOUT_SAME_SIGN op. An assessor-flagged overclaim is never a finding, nor written into a finding's statement or next_test: at most a question with honest numbers. A round's step over its own parent (nested waves) has no t of its own: it goes into the finding's next_test or a question, never into a new finding and never as evidence for a dial.
3. Mechanism names: only those in {PACK}/MECHANISMS.txt (alarm pack: FORCED_OUT, FORCED_OUT_SHORTS, CROWDED_LATE; its hunters use only the first two until CROWDED_LATE has a yardstick). A market condition is a STATE or CONDITION_EFFECT finding inside a mechanism, never a mechanism; the tool refuses UNKNOWN_MECHANISM. Knowledge rows name states and sizes, never a trade direction.
4. Moves (memory runs): one `$K op --op MOVE --target <move> --set mechanism= eid= beat_r1=1|0 gain_vs_r1= gain_vs_forecast= fail=1|0 note=` per change of the fitted-change round (alarm R3; the pack's round map names its round), an under-300-wave one too (waves in the note). A ladder of settings tried in that round is one change: one op with the eid of the rung the card names best (the other rungs stay evidence rows; their gains in the note). beat_r1=1 when that rung's net beats its R1 on the same trades; gain_vs_forecast = gain_vs_r1 minus the hunter's sealed gain forecast. fail=1 only when the try made things worse: beat_r1=0, or its net minus the yardstick is 0 or below; a try that beats both but misses another pass part (t, months, robustness) is fail=0, the missed part in the note. None for a placebo: a placebo is not a move.
5. Questions (memory runs): each line of the card's NEXT TESTS becomes one OPEN_QUESTIONS row (would_move_fid: a finding id F...; test_sketch; priority); any other new question only with its reason in CURATE.tsv; a question this run took gets answered_by_eid when its test answered it, else it stays TAKEN with the reason. A market question stays a question until a test answers it.
6. Citations (memory runs, through the UPDATE op): for each F or Q id the run's CLAIMS cite, the run goes into cited_by_runs with its margin against the same-side yardstick.
7. `$K check` (rc 0 needed), `$K brief`, then the pooled split check (`python3 -B {PACK}/tools/pool_splits_v1.py`); into RUNF/ops/curator_scripts/KNOW_V1_AFTER.txt: the CHECK line, the brief's real sha16 (shasum -a 256 | cut -c1-16) and the pooled check's result lines themselves (each reading with its pooled gap and t), not only a path.
8. Proposals: for every row of {REGISTERS}/PROPOSALS.tsv whose latest status is PROPOSED and that was logged before your launch (skip rows marked "fresh checker" that are about your own skill or prompt), add one status row (its id: the prop_id plus a letter, e.g. PX01a): CHECKED or REJECTED with one line why (real, new, one part, measurable, safe). A proposal about your own skill or prompt: a status row PROPOSED with check_note "fresh checker", and leave it.
