## One answer for a mixed result: each dimension goes on by its own gates
The label is the first gate of each dimension, not the handoff. Hand over only what passed all its own gates, and write which claim and why. A claim that fails or lacks a gate is dropped from the handoff; the other claim still goes.
| dimension | goes on when all hold | failed | not yet measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| SIZE | first look's SIZE both periods; new as a SIZE claim (timing) | not handed over: its reason | waits |
| SIDE | first look's SIDE both periods; new as a SIDE claim; MONTH HELD or STRONGER in 3 of 4 | not handed over: its reason | waits |
- SIZE+SIDE with new timing and only two qualifying SIDE months: hand over SIZE alone; SIDE stops at the monthly gate, and the strategy must bring its own side.
- SIZE with identical fires and NEW SIDE: hand over nothing. The timing is a copy, so SIZE stops; NEW SIDE says nothing of timing, and the table shows no SIDE to carry.
- Missing evidence is never a pass and never a fail: a gate not run keeps that dimension waiting, as NEEDS MORE MONTHS (x) or "novelty not run", and the report says which gate.
- A novelty result covers only the registry and the siblings actually checked: name them. It never says new against everything; the registry is not reconciled.

## An unsigned SIZE idea
The queue needs FADE or FOLLOW on every variant: its lists and races need a side. For an unsigned SIZE idea register one as a TECHNICAL LEAN: write in the VARIANTS `reason` "TECHNICAL LEAN, no side claimed"; report SIDE and R1, R2 as information only, and never hand over SIDE.
A directional STOP from decide_v4 does not end SIZE, but it does block MONTH, and a FULL first look needs that MONTH job. So with a STOP and no authorized later source, keep NEEDS MORE MONTHS (SIZE) and stop there: that is the answer. A March-only brief does not authorize the January to April batch MONTH runs. `--forward-only` tests a different claim, the other side, and is not a way round the STOP.

Not money: money is the strategy's. Thin or unread months are printed as such, never as a fail. Edition 12's SIZE waits on its review and fix before it is the bar in force; edition 11's is printed beside it.
